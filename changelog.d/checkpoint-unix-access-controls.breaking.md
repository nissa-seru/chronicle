- **Linux and macOS checkpoint writers:** Atomic metadata replacement now requires readable regular checkpoint files and permission to restore their owner, group, mode, and per-file ACL. Symlink destinations are rejected. Linux also requires readable POSIX ACL/security-xattr APIs and matching initial security labels on staging files; unfamiliar system ACL attributes and content-bound IMA/EVM signatures are rejected. macOS rejects `com.apple.macl` authorization labels. Before upgrading a writer, confirm that its account and filesystem can preserve the store's existing policy; unsupported policy causes saves to fail rather than being discarded. Checkpoint file encodings are unchanged and remain compatible with existing readers.
- Replacements retain Linux POSIX ACLs, macOS extended ACLs (including inherited entries), and visible Linux security-label xattrs and capabilities. Replacement files stay private while their contents are written, and failures to preserve supported controls retain the previous checkpoint. Path-based MAC policy must cover both final checkpoint names and `.chronicle-checkpoint-*` staging names; attributes hidden by the filesystem from the caller are outside the preservation contract.
